# Privacy Policy – Live Call Translator

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Live Call Translator ("the app") helps people who speak different Indian languages talk to each other by translating speech in real time.

## What the app uses
- **Microphone** – only while you are on the Interpreter or Translated Call screen, to convert your speech into text. Audio is processed by your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google). The app itself does not record or store audio.
- **Internet** – to download offline language packs, to send translated-call text to the other participant, and (optionally) to translate via Sarvam AI.

## What data leaves your phone
- **Translated Call mode:** the recognized text of what you say, your display name and your language are sent through our relay server to the other people in the same room code. The server only forwards messages in real time; it does **not** store, log or share conversation text.
- **Sarvam AI (optional):** if you enter your own Sarvam AI API key, the text to be translated is sent to Sarvam AI (sarvam.ai) under their privacy policy.
- **Offline translation:** Google ML Kit translation runs on your device; text is not sent to a server.

## What we do not do
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- No contacts, location, SMS or call logs are accessed.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties beyond those listed above.

## Data stored on your phone
Your language choices, display name, server address and optional API key are saved only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.
